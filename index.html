THE BRIDE

Tanya Mathew

D/o Late Dr Abhraham K Mathew & Reena Mathew

An advocate by profession, Tanya has mastered the fine art of arguing a case so convincingly that Justin sometimes forgets what the original argument was about. She believes every disagreement deserves a proper hearing, preferably with supporting evidence, while Justin believes pretending to agree is an excellent legal strategy. Between keeping the law in order and keeping Justin on his toes, she somehow still finds time to be the one person he willingly lets win.

THE GROOM

Justin Moncy

S/o Mr Moncy Varghese & Lissy Moncy

An ecologist by profession, Justin spends his days studying ecosystems and the delicate balance of nature — which is excellent preparation for being married to Tanya. He firmly believes in observing problems before attempting to solve them, much to the frustration of the person who would like him to solve them immediately. Tanya calls it procrastination; Justin calls it scientific observation. Together, they have established a perfectly sustainable ecosystem where one argues, one overthinks, and somehow both end up laughing.
